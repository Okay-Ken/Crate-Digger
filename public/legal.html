Terms of Service

Crate Digger helps you find where to buy or download the tracks in your playlists. It builds search links to third-party music stores and checks public catalog information. It does not sell, host, or download music. All purchases happen on the stores' own sites, under their terms.

Pro subscription. Crate Digger Pro costs $5 per month, billed monthly through our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, until you cancel. Your license key works while your subscription is active. Keys are for your own use; sharing a key may get it disabled.

Fair use. Don't use Crate Digger to overload our service or the services it connects to, or for anything illegal. We may suspend access that abuses the service.

Changes. We may update these terms. If a change affects your subscription, we'll post it here before it takes effect.

Privacy Policy

What we store. Your track list and store settings are saved in your own browser. If you have Pro, your track list is also saved on our server under a scrambled version of your license key, so it syncs across your devices. We don't ask for your name to use the site.

Playlist links. When you import a playlist, we send the link to YouTube or SoundCloud to read the track names. Track names are sent to Apple's iTunes search to check availability. We don't keep a log of the playlists you import.

Payments. Lemon Squeezy handles checkout and billing and receives your email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email and subscription status, not your card number.

Hosting. The site runs on Netlify, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security.

Your choices. You can clear your list any time with the Clear button. To have your saved Pro list deleted, contact us with your license key.

Refund Policy

You can cancel anytime from the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, and you keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for. Since you can cancel whenever you like, we don't refund partial months. If something isn't working or you were charged by mistake, contact us within 14 days and we'll make it right, including a refund when it's warranted.
