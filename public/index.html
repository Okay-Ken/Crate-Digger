Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, or a link that won't load? Do this instead
  1. Open TuneMyMusic.com and tap Let's Start.
  2. Pick where your playlist lives (Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, YouTube Music…), sign in, and choose the playlist.
  3. For the destination, choose Export to file and save it as a TXT or CSV file.
  4. Come back here and tap Load .txt / .csv below, or copy the song names and paste them in the box.

Spotify only: Exportify.net also gives you a CSV file that works here. Spotify Wrapped and YouTube Music Recap playlists are private, so they always need this route.
